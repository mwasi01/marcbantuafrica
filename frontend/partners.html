Who We Partner With

Banks & MFIs

Use our farm records to power credit scoring and loan decisions for smallholder farmers.

Insurers

Offer crop and livestock insurance based on verified farm data and production history.

Input Suppliers

Reach farmers directly with seeds, fertilizer, and chemicals via our platform.

Cooperatives

Manage members, aggregate produce, and share resources with our platform.

NGOs & Donors

Track farmer impact, measure adoption, and report on program outcomes.

Buyers & Processors

Source quality produce from verified farmers with traceable records.

Why Partner With Marcbantu

  • Reach — access thousands of smallholder farmers across Africa
  • Data — anonymized, aggregated farm data for decision-making
  • Integration — API access to records, transactions, credit scores
  • Trust — farmers own their data; they control what's shared
  • Multi-channel — connect via web, SMS, USSD, or WhatsApp
  • Impact — measurable improvements in farmer income and productivity
