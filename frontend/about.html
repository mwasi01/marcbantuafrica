About Marcbantu Africa

Empowering African farmers to run their farms like profitable businesses.

Our Mission

Marcbantu Africa exists to help small and medium-scale farmers across the continent move from guesswork to data-driven farming. We believe that with the right tools and knowledge, every African farmer can run a profitable, sustainable farm business.

What We Do

We combine three things that most farm management tools don't offer together:

  • Farm management principles — clear planning, record keeping, and decision-making frameworks
  • Simple digital tools — easy-to-use software that works on any phone, online or offline
  • Pan-African context — built for the realities of African farming, from feature phones to mobile money

Who We Serve

Our platform is designed for:

  • Small-scale farmers looking to increase yields and profits
  • Agripreneurs building commercial farm businesses
  • Farm managers optimizing daily operations
  • Cooperatives and farmer groups managing members and shared resources

Our Approach

We combine global best practices in farm management with African realities. Our platform is available through:

  • Mobile web app (PWA) — works on any smartphone, installable, offline-capable
  • SMS — market prices, weather alerts, reminders on any phone
  • USSD — no internet needed for basic operations
  • WhatsApp — record activities, get reports, in your chat
  • Voice — for low-literacy farmers

Our Values

  • Simplicity — complex problems, simple solutions
  • Accessibility — works on any phone, in any language
  • Profitability — we measure success by farmer income growth
  • Trust — your data is yours. Always.
